05   Strategic advising

Churn risk scoring

The XGBoost model outputs a calibrated probability [0,1] per player. Players above a chosen threshold are flagged as high-risk and routed to downstream retention workflows.

Personalized re-engagement

High-risk players receive notifications tailored to their behavioral profile — genre preferences, playtime patterns, and social activity — rather than generic promotions.

Competing game recommendations

Players at risk of leaving a title are surfaced alternatives within the same genre cluster, reducing platform-level churn even when game-level churn is unavoidable.

Threshold tuning

The classification threshold is a business lever — stricter thresholds cut false positives (fewer wasted interventions), looser thresholds maximize recall (catch more at-risk players).

06   Retention levers
🎯
Behavioral triggers
Inactivity windows and declining session frequency are the strongest churn signals. Trigger interventions at day 7 and day 14 of inactivity before the player fully disengages.
💬
Social re-engagement
Players with high votes_up and comment_count are community-invested. Highlight friend activity, new reviews, and events to pull them back.
📊
Platform-level retention
When a player churns from one game, recommend within-platform alternatives ranked by similarity — keeping cumulative playtime on Steam even as game-level loyalty fades.
