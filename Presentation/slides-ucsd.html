WHY PLAYER RETENTION?
Motivation & Problem Statement
Steam hosts over 50,000 games and 132 million active users — understanding churn is critical for publishers maximizing lifetime value.
Most players who churn do so within the first 7 days, making early detection essential for timely intervention.
Existing approaches rely on aggregate metrics — we combine behavioral logs, text, and metadata for a multi-modal signal.
Our system predicts churn probability and recommends competing games — closing the re-engagement loop.

EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS
Feature Distributions: Playtime, session frequency, and review counts show strong right skew — most players have minimal histories.
Correlation Analysis: Session length and frequency are most correlated with 30-day retention. Social connections amplify this effect.
Temporal Patterns: Churn signatures are detectable within the first login session using early behavioral proxies.
Engagement Tiers: High-engagement cohort shows 3× lower churn within 30 days versus low-engagement cohort.

KEY FINDINGS & RECOMMENDATIONS
01
Early Churn Detection
Players who churn within 7 days show distinct behavioral signatures at first login — enabling proactive intervention.
02
Session Quality > Quantity
Session length is more predictive than total session count — deeper engagement per visit drives long-term retention.
03
Recommendation Re-engagement
Competing game recommendations via collaborative filtering improve re-engagement rates at high-risk churn moments.
04
Scalable Personalization
SVD++ collaborative filtering achieves personalization on minimal interaction data — effective for cold-start players.
