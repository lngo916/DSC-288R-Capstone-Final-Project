Project Overview
Why Player Retention?
Our project investigates how behavioral gameplay patterns, review sentiment, and engagement frequency can be used to predict player churn on Steam. Using large-scale interaction data and machine learning models including XGBoost, Random Forest, and LSTMs, we analyze retention drivers, engineer predictive behavioral features, and evaluate model performance through precision, recall, and AUC-ROC metrics.
1.2M+
Player Profiles
Behavioral logs, session data, and purchase histories across thousands of Steam titles.
Data Scale
11K+
Steam Games
Multi-genre metadata, tags, pricing, user reviews, and release info from the Kaggle Steam dataset.
Dataset

ML Models
XGBoost, Random Forest, LSTM, and Collaborative Filtering for churn prediction and recommendations.

AUC-ROC Score
Best performing model achieves 91% AUC-ROC, 87% precision, and 83% recall on the held-out test set.

Actionable Insights
01
Session length and frequency are the strongest predictors of long-term retention — prioritize early engagement loops.
02
Players who churn within 7 days share distinct early behavioral patterns detectable at first login.
03
Competing game recommendations improve re-engagement rates by surfacing relevant alternatives at risk moments.
04
Personalization at scale is achievable with collaborative filtering on minimal interaction data via SVD++.
